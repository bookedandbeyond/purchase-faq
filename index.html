🛒 Before You Buy

Before You Buy

What exactly am I buying?▼
You are buying a complete Client Experience System for a specific destination series. Each system includes pre-written email templates, pre-built automations, advisor tasks, and a destination workflow, all designed to import directly into Tern. You also receive a branded setup guide (PDF) and access to the online advisor resource guide.
What series are available?▼
Four series are currently available: Western Europe (France, Paris, French Riviera, Monaco, Belgium, Netherlands), British Isles (United Kingdom, London, Scotland, Ireland), Mediterranean (Italy, Croatia, Spain, Portugal, Greece), and Hawaii (Oahu, Maui, Kauai, Big Island, Lanai, Multi-Island).
Do I need Tern to use this?▼
Yes. The system is built exclusively for Tern. The email templates, automations, and workflows are formatted for Tern's import and automation features. They cannot be used in other CRMs without manual recreation.
Is this a subscription or a one-time purchase?▼
It is a one-time purchase. You pay once per series and own the content. Annual updates to email content and new templates added to your series are included at no additional cost.
Can I buy individual destinations instead of a full series?▼
Series are sold as complete packages. Individual destination workflows are not sold separately because the shared emails (Packing List, Currency, VAT, Entry Requirements) are designed to work across the full series.
Do you offer a bundle discount?▼
Check the Booked & Beyond shop for current bundle pricing. Bundles that include multiple series or the full collection are periodically available at a discount.

💳 Purchasing & Access

Purchasing & Access

How do I purchase?▼
Visit the Booked & Beyond shop on Podia, select your series, and complete checkout. You receive instant access to your download and setup guide.
What happens after I buy?▼
You receive a confirmation email from Podia with a link to access your purchase. Your downloads include the Tern workflow file, your series setup guide (PDF), and access to the online advisor resource guide. The workflow also appears in your Tern Library under Shared with Me.
How do I access my purchase later?▼
Log in to your Podia account at any time to re-download your files. Your purchase does not expire.
Can I get a refund?▼
Because the product includes downloadable digital files and immediate access to templates in Tern, refunds are generally not offered once the content has been accessed or downloaded. If you experience a technical issue that prevents you from using the product, reach out to katie@bookedandbeyond.co and we will work with you to resolve it.
I did not receive my confirmation email. What should I do?▼
Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it is not there, log in to your Podia account directly to access your purchases. If you still cannot find it, email katie@bookedandbeyond.co with your order details.

📦 What's Included

What's Included

How many email templates are in each series?▼
It varies by series. Western Europe includes 14 templates, British Isles includes 15+, Mediterranean includes 22+, and Hawaii includes 8. Each template is a complete, ready-to-send email with subject line and body copy.
Are the emails generic or destination-specific?▼
Every email is destination-specific. Must See/Do emails cover the sights, food, and experiences unique to each city or region. Currency emails reference the local currency and tipping customs. Packing lists are tailored to the climate and culture. Nothing is generic filler.
Do the templates include images or just text?▼
Templates include styled, branded text content ready for Tern's email builder. Images and banners can be added in Tern's editor when you personalize your copy.
What are the advisor tasks?▼
Most workflows include two built-in tasks that appear on your Tern task list after you apply the workflow: one reminds you to schedule the visa or entry-requirement email, and another reminds you to review and activate automation timings. They are simple reminders, not automated emails.
Are updates included?▼
Yes. When email content is updated (for example, if a destination's entry requirements change or new content is added), the updated templates are pushed to your Tern Library. Annual content refreshes are included with your purchase at no extra cost.
Can I see a sample before I buy?▼
The demo packet on the Booked & Beyond website shows a full five-email Iceland sequence as a sample. It illustrates the style, depth, and structure of every destination's emails.

🚀 Using Your Purchase

Using Your Purchase

How do I import the workflow into Tern?▼
Your workflow appears in Tern under Workflows, then Shared with Me. Open the destination folder, click the workflow, and choose Make a Copy to add it to your personal library. From there, apply it to any trip.
Can I customize the emails?▼
Yes. Make a Copy of any template in your Tern Library before editing. Rename your copy, make changes (add affiliate links, adjust wording, insert images), and swap it into the workflow using the three-dot menu on the automation. Your edits propagate to every trip using that template.
Can I use these emails outside of Tern?▼
The content is yours, so you can reference or adapt the copy for other uses. However, the automations, trigger timings, and workflow structure are built specifically for Tern and would need to be manually recreated in another platform.
Can I share my purchase with another advisor?▼
Each purchase is licensed for a single user or agency. Sharing login credentials, forwarding workflow files, or distributing templates to other advisors is not permitted. If a colleague wants to use the system, they need their own purchase.
I sell a destination that is not in any series. Can I request one?▼
Yes. Reach out to katie@bookedandbeyond.co with the destination. Booked & Beyond is actively developing new series and destination requests help prioritize what gets built next.
How do Amazon affiliate links work with the templates?▼
Three types of emails include placeholder spots for your Amazon Associates links: Inspiration emails (link to your storefront), Must See/Do emails (link to guidebooks), and Packing emails (link to recommended products). Replace the placeholders with your own affiliate links to earn commissions on client purchases. Amazon Associates is free to join.

🛠 Technical & Compatibility

Technical & Compatibility

What CRM is this compatible with?▼
Tern only. The workflows, automations, and templates are formatted for Tern's system. They are not compatible with TravelJoy, Traveler's Q, or other travel CRMs without manual recreation.
Will this work on my phone or tablet?▼
Tern's web interface works on any device with a browser. You can review automations and toggle statuses from your phone, though initial setup is easier on a desktop or laptop.
What if Tern changes their platform?▼
Booked & Beyond monitors Tern updates and adjusts workflows and templates as needed. If a Tern update affects your system, updated versions are pushed to your library.
I am having a technical issue with Tern. Can you help?▼
For issues specific to Tern's platform (login, syncing, general CRM questions), contact Tern support directly. For issues with the Booked & Beyond workflows, templates, or automations, reach out to katie@bookedandbeyond.co.
